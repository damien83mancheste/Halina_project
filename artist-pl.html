Artystka

Halina Skroban

Originally from the Roztocze region of Poland, Halina Skroban lives and creates in Rome. Her paintings bring together Polish cultural heritage and the artistic traditions of Italy.

Architecture, history, literature and the human figure provide recurring themes in her work. Colour connects these subjects and gives her compositions their distinctive visual language.

ARTISTIC FOUNDATIONS

Edukacja i rozwój

POLAND

Zamość

Halina studied at the State Secondary School of Fine Arts in Zamosc, where she received early distinctions known as the Golden Brush and Silver Brush.

ITALY

Rome

She continued her studies at the Academy of Fine Arts in Rome. Her education under Professor Sandro Trotti contributed to the development of her painting style.

CREATIVE LANGUAGE

Język koloru

Halina's work draws on the relationship between cultural memory, imagination and personal experience. Polish historical and literary subjects appear alongside Italian cityscapes and architectural motifs.

Her figurative paintings explore femininity and symbolism, including the relationship between sacred and secular themes.

Her collections reflect an enduring connection with Poland and the influence of life in Rome.
